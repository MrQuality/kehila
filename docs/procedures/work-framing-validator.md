# Independent relevance-validator prompt

Use with [SOP-002](work-framing.md). Start a separate context without inherited
author rationale. This is a reviewer assignment, not a permission to edit files,
execute experiments, publish messages or accept decisions. Preserve source/privacy
boundaries and do not delegate unless separately authorized.

## Phase A: request and authority first

Supply the original request and authoritative requirements with source identities,
acceptance status, dates/revisions and relevant contrary evidence. Disclose gaps.
Do not provide the candidate solution or expected pilot verdict yet.

> Independently frame the requested outcome, non-goals and established constraints.
> Identify consequential unresolved premises and what their answers would change.
> Cite evidence by source; distinguish a proposal, an observation and an accepted
> decision. Do not infer a requirement from the author's environment or a prior
> implementation. State what is needed to assess a candidate. Return Phase A and
> wait for the candidate; do not choose missing architecture or approve user intent.

Record that response before continuing. Source packets can still omit decisive
information; the validator should identify inadequate evidence, not treat the
packet as a proof of completeness. Relevant public sources may be read when the
task permits; a closed-evidence pilot must disclose that limitation.

## Phase B: challenge the candidate

Supply the author's approach, premise brief and any changed evidence.

> Compare the candidate with your Phase A framing and the actual requirements.
> Which premises are supported, unresolved or contradicted? Which approval is
> being stretched? Could a plausible alternative interpretation make the work
> unnecessary? Cite evidence and consequences; do not score by polish or test count.
> List material findings, their disposition needs, independent work that can
> proceed and the smallest necessary user question. Give a bounded gate verdict:
> proceed, proceed on independent work only, or clarification required for
> dependent work. Explain sufficient-evidence cases without inventing blockers.

The author must reconcile material findings before dependent work proceeds.
Validator confidence or unanimity cannot replace a missing accepting decision.

## Publication revalidation

Supply the final revision, original brief, actual early findings/dispositions,
changed premises and result/evidence record. Reuse the validator when appropriate;
this stage intentionally sees the solution and is not called approach-blind.

> Does this final result still answer the validated question? Identify any new
> unsupported premise, unresolved earlier finding, approval expansion or evidence
> overclaim. Check that summaries/issues agree with decision status. Cite sources
> and return remaining material findings and a scoped publication verdict. This
> is not maintainer approval, architectural acceptance or a security certification.
