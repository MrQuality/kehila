# Milestone / large-goal retrospective

Use at the closeout boundaries defined by [SOP-003](engineering-delivery.md).
Keep it proportional; reuse the task/PR evidence. Do not turn it into another
qualification campaign. Recurrence and controls are accepted; exact SOP wording
and demonstrated effectiveness remain separate matters.

- Milestone/goal, owner, source interval and delivery/PR identity:
- Original outcome; explicit scope changes and their authority:
- Actual result, unresolved work, stopped/deferred cases and evidence limits:
- Commit/publication, decision and failure timeline (source-backed dates):
- What worked and should remain:

| Observation and supporting source | Category / consequence | Established cause or bounded hypothesis | Improvement / priority / owner | Acceptance / implementation state | Next observation / validity limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Concrete evidence; retain failed attempts | Product, harness, environment or process | Do not infer hours or counterfactual prevention | Specific, proportional action | Proposed, accepted, implemented, exercised or effectiveness unqualified | No invented target |

- Timing/resource summary: measured spans, unknowns, overlaps and excluded work.
- Risk controls: residual risk, owner/acceptance authority and material changes.
- Independent challenge: original request/evidence inspected, findings/dispositions.
- Synchronization: task/PR/issues/docs and approved process/skill changes.
- Follow-up at next applicable milestone/large-goal closeout; do not add an extra
  project gate to obtain long-term effectiveness evidence.
